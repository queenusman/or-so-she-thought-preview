The Premise

Or So She Thought

Some lives seem to have been decided long before they begin.

Or So She Thought is a novel about a young woman growing up in a world that has given her almost everything: a loving family, a place of privilege, a kingdom rich in history, and a future that has always seemed certain. Yet there is a difference between knowing the life expected of you and discovering the life you actually want to live.

Set in Norwyn, an ancient kingdom where the Crown passes through the female line and centuries of tradition shape even the most ordinary parts of modern life, the story follows Princess Elisabeta through the familiar rhythms of royal life. Between family gatherings, personal friendships, royal obligations, unexpected encounters, and the quiet complexities of growing up, she begins to notice things she once accepted without question.

The people closest to her are not always as easy to understand as she imagined. Familiar relationships reveal unexpected depths, small moments take on new meaning, and the future she has always known begins to look different from the one she might have chosen.

Because sometimes, the greatest changes do not begin with a rebellion or a dramatic decision. They begin with a closer look at the life you have always known.

And Elisabeta thought she knew hers.

A Note from the Author

Why I Wrote This Story

Welcome to Or So She Thought.

I have always been fascinated by people: the lives they build, the relationships they hold close, the things they leave unsaid, and the little ways they change without always realising it. I am drawn to stories that take the time to notice these things, where a seemingly ordinary conversation, a familiar face, or a small moment can reveal something much larger about a person.

That curiosity is part of what brought Norwyn to life.

I wanted to create a world that felt lived in, not simply one that existed for the sake of a story. A place with its own history, customs, households, institutions, and changing seasons. A kingdom where people have their own concerns, private joys, complicated relationships, and lives that continue beyond the moments we see on the page.

And at the centre of it all is Elisabeta, whose world I hope you will enjoy getting to know.

There is no need to hurry. Take your time exploring Norwyn, wander through its archives, meet its people, and discover the story at your own pace. I hope you find something here that makes you pause, smile, wonder, or see something a little differently.

Thank you for being here. It is lovely to finally share this world with you.

— Queen Usman
