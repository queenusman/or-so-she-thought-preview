The Premise

The Premise

Before the Curtain Rises

Or So She Thought is a novel about a young woman shaped by an ancient kingdom, a loving family, and a future that seems settled long before she has lived it.

Set in Norwyn—a land where the Crown passes through the female line and where tradition lives beside modern life—the story follows Princess Elisabeta as ordinary days, quiet observations, and long-held assumptions begin to shift beneath familiar routines.

At its heart is a quiet question: what happens when someone begins to see the world—and her own place within it—more clearly than she ever thought to look?

A Note from the Author

A Note from the Author

Why I Wrote This Story

Welcome to the world of Or So She Thought.

For a long time, I have been drawn to stories that unfold quietly—stories about the small moments in which a person begins to notice the weight of expectation, the comfort of what is familiar, and the distance between what others see and what lies within.

Norwyn grew slowly: its seasons, its institutions, its households, and the people who inhabit them. Take your time as you read, explore the world and its archives at your own pace, and step into the chapters whenever you are ready.

— Queen Usman
