Or So She Thought

Set in 1990s Norwyn, where duty is inherited and futures are arranged before they are questioned. A story about what happens when a young woman begins to look closely at the life she was prepared to want.

01 — The Web Novel

The Life She Is Supposed to Want

Elizabeth has spent most of her life knowing what is expected of her. As a royal, a student, and a future bride, she has learned how to read a room and understand what a situation requires. But across 26 meticulously written chapters, the certainty of her world begins to unravel.

Featuring Edward of Valenne, Elias, and the quiet weight of expectation.

02 — The World

Explore the Kingdom of Norwyn

A kingdom shaped by inheritance, tradition, commerce, and the people who have learned how to live within them. From the busy stone quays of the capital to the quiet administrative halls of the palace and the rugged mountains beyond.

03 — Characters

Meet the People of Norwyn

They love her. They need things from her. They protect her. They misunderstand her. None of them can be reduced to what they want from Elizabeth.

Elizabeth

A daughter, royal, student, and future bride who knows how to anticipate what a situation requires—until quiet compliance starts to feel like an exhausting form of labor.

Queen Anne

Elizabeth's mother, driven by duty and a fierce belief that discipline is the highest form of protection, blurring the line between devotion and constraint.

Edward of Valenne

The man Elizabeth is expected to marry. Intelligent, observant, and politically aware, he brings consequences far beyond a personal arrangement.

Elias

Coming from a world outside palace walls, Elias is independent, observant, and unimpressed by the rigid distinctions of the royal household.

07 — The Author

About Queen Usman

Queen Usman is a Nigerian clinical medical student and novelist[cite: 1]. She writes fiction about the things that shape people before they fully understand they are being shaped: family, desire, expectation, relationships, power, circumstance, and repetition[cite: 1].

Her medical training has sharpened her interest in observation, human behavior, and the spaces between what happens to a person and what they eventually become[cite: 1].
