The Inherited World • Elisabeta’s Story

Born into the royal family of Norwyn, Princess Elisabeta has never had to imagine a life outside the one prepared for her. Her world has its own order, its traditions, its expectations and its understanding of what makes a good life. Within it, almost everything has an explanation, and almost every decision has its proper place.

She has grown up knowing what is expected of her, what her future will look like, and what it means to belong to the family that will one day shape the kingdom's future.

But as marriage, family relationships and the responsibilities of the crown begin to bring those expectations into sharper focus, Elisabeta finds herself confronting questions she has never had much reason to ask.

What happens when the life you have always understood begins to look different from the inside? When does duty become something you believe in, and when is it simply something you have learned to accept? And how do you distinguish between what you genuinely want and what you have spent your life learning to want?

Her difficulty is not simply deciding whether to follow the life expected of her or turn away from it. It is discovering how much of her understanding of herself, her family and the world has been formed by the very life she is beginning to question.

Record #0012-A • Treaty of the First Stones

Edict on the Consanguine League of Valenne

"Let it be inscribed: Henceforth, Norwyn and the House of Valenne shall govern under marital concord. The royal heir shall betroth the direct issue of Valenne at their eighteenth summer, assuring unbroken sovereignty and the defense of the northern trade gates."

Penciled Marginalia: Princess Elisabeta (Royal Archives Folio)

"They recite this clause to me as though the ink were mixed with mortar. Every tutor speaks of unbroken sovereignty, yet none can tell me why an emergency bargain struck generations ago requires my own hands to seal it anew."

Record #0087-C • Royal Nursery Directive

Memorandum on Elisabeta’s Schedule

"The Princess should be instructed in continental cadence, state economics, and music. Under no circumstance should her schedule permit unregulated retreat. Solitude invites melancholy in future rulers; purpose is the greatest armor a daughter of Norwyn can wear."

Ecaterina's Note to the Governess (Found in private desk)

"Do not let her think this is punishment. I spent my eighteenth year trapped in a carriage while councilors debated whether I was sane enough to inherit. If Elisabeta knows the answer before they can frame the question, nobody will ever dare question her mind. Keep her busy so she does not bleed."
