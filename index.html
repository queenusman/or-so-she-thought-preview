Current issue

Issue No. 1 — The Unreliable Narrator

Nine stories and a suite of poems built around the moment a narrator stops being able to vouch for their own account — the misremembered room, the corrected diary entry, the confession that changes shape halfway through. Paired with a new series of ink studies commissioned for this issue.

Commissioned artwork by Reiko Sato

Reserve Issue No. 1
We publish the version of the story a person tells right before they correct themselves.

— from the founding note, Issue No. 1

What we publish

  • Fiction Dark literary short fiction, generally under 6,000 words, with room for the occasional novella-length piece in a themed issue. 01
  • Poetry Poems that sit with dread, grief, and doubt rather than resolve them — up to five poems per submission. 02
  • Criticism Essays and reviews on dark fiction past and present, from working critics and working writers alike. 03
  • Visual art Each issue pairs its fiction with commissioned artwork made in direct response to the accepted pieces. 04

Submissions open twice a year

We read fiction, poetry, and criticism during two windows a year. We ask for a two-week response outside of those windows, and no simultaneous withdrawal fees — just tell us if a piece is placed elsewhere.

Read the submission guidelines
Winter window — Jan 1 – Jan 31
Summer window — Jun 1 – Jun 30
Response time — 8–10 weeks
